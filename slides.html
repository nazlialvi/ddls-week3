01 · The question

Can ESR1 give the tumour board a trustworthy ER-positive shortlist?

Dr. Camille Dubois wanted an explainable second opinion on recorded ER status for 198 tumours, to prioritise limited endocrine-therapy review — not diagnosis.

What she already believed: “ESR1 is the obvious biological reading to examine first.” She asked whether “ESR1 alone gives a clear, trustworthy separation, or whether several oestrogen-programme genes are needed.”

Her requested output was a clear ESR1 cut-off, label counts on either side, a ranked shortlist, and an honest test of whether adding genes genuinely improved ESR1 alone.

Decision: simple, defensible ER-status review prioritisation1 / 6

The observed differences are not statistically distinguishable from chance at this sample size; JRH’s 11/24 ER-positive rate is consistent with sampling noise.

I refused to claim: that a non-significant result proves the model is free of centre bias. It does not.

06 · Limitations + AI disclosure

What the result cannot tell us yet

Biological caveat: clinical ER status and ESR1 mRNA expression measure different biological layers. Imperfect concordance can deflate the margin and limit generalisation.

Also missing: independent validation. With another week: obtain probe mapping, batch/processing history, collection dates, filtering criteria and an independent cohort; pre-specify threshold, review capacity and borderline handling.

AI-use disclosure

Pi built the environment, ran the analysis, wrote the result files/report and built the FastAPI/Tailwind dashboard.

I verified and corrected: the undefined-headline bug; raw HTML leaking in the gene table; unwired gene-click rows; and the wrong ESR1-alone number that initially overstated the four-gene advantage. A live re-check caught the error and established the exact 175/198 tie.

The dashboard’s live API/assert checks verified the toggle’s updated headline comparison and the expression-detail path for every shortlist gene.

Plain truth: explainable second opinion, not clinical replacement6 / 6
